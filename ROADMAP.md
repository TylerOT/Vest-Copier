# Roadmap

What I'm working on for the copier. No dates, and plans can change. Once something ships, it moves to the
[changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## Next

- **More from the chart's right-click menu:** place a limit or a stop order at the price you clicked.
- **Share a trade when it closes.** A quick prompt to post it.

## Later

- **Pick your own colors.** Buy and sell colors and the theme's, in the Appearance settings.

## Have an idea?

Open a [feature request](https://github.com/xAmped/Vest-Copier/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) or drop it in
the [Discord](https://discord.gg/Aa69y9KnM3).
