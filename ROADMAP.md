# Roadmap

What I'm working on for the copier. No dates, and plans can change. Once something ships, it moves to the
[changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## Next

- **Clearer P&L wording.** "Closed" becomes "Realized" (fees, funding and closed trades), and "you keep" is upfront
  about the cost of closing.
- **Bigger stop and target numbers** on the Trade tab.
- **Share a trade when it closes.** A quick prompt to post it.

## Later

- **Appearance settings.** Pick your own buy and sell colors, theme colors and text size, all in one place.
- **Stop entries (testing).** Vest only has market and limit entries. The idea: mark a price on the chart and the
  copier buys or sells at market when price gets there, like a buy stop or sell stop. Testing first.
